# Support

mudroom-support is maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no SLA and no guaranteed response time.

- **Bugs and questions**: open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/mudroom-ai/mudroom-support/issues/new/choose). Include the version or commit, the connector and the steps to reproduce.
- **Security**: do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Start with the [docs](docs/README.md): [configuration](docs/configuration.md), [deploy](docs/deploy.md), [errors](docs/errors.md) and the [connector docs](docs/connectors/README.md).

The server runs on your own host, so problems with your server, proxy, Slack workspace or Resend account are yours to debug. See [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md).
